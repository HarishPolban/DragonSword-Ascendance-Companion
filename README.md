## 🔮 Vision & Philosophy

A lot of tool-sets treat the player as a passenger. The Twilight Codex Companion treats you as the cartographer. Our design pillars rest on three ideas that shape every line of the project:

**1. Reversibility is sacred.** Every adjustment you make is non-destructive. Toggle anything on or off, restore defaults with a single tap, and experiment freely — the realm always remembers how to return to its original shape.

**2. Visibility over mystery.** Advancement, timings, resource flows, and hidden modifiers are surfaced in elegant dashboards. When numbers stop hiding, players make better decisions.

**3. Play your way, at your pace.** Some love blistering combat loops. Others savor slow walks through twilight valleys. The companion bows to both.

The result is a companion that feels less like a toolbox and more like a trusted squire — always nearby, never intrusive.

## 🧩 Module Deep Dive

### ⚔️ Combat Module

Combat in **DragonSword: Awakening** is a dance of anticipation. The Combat Module refines that dance with surgical precision, offering adjustable parameters for enemy aggression cadence, parry window comfort, and stamina economy. Turn a brutal duel into a teaching moment, or sharpen every encounter into a razor's edge challenge. Sub-features include:

- Adjustable reaction windows for defensive maneuvers.
- Enemy pacing curves that scale from contemplative to relentless.
- Damage feedback visualization with per-hit telemetry readouts.
- Combo-chain stability tuning for players who prefer pattern experimentation.

### 🏃 Movement Module

Movement is the poetry between battles. This module lets you sculpt that poetry — from traversal acceleration to fall forgiveness, from sprint stamina drain to swimming fluidity. Ideal for players who enjoy gliding through landscapes like ink through water.

- Customizable dash and sprint behavior.
- Environmental traversal aids for vertical exploration.
- Subtle camera stabilization during rapid transitions.
- Terrain interaction smoothing for uneven surfaces.

### 🧭 Exploration Module

The world is vast, and the Exploration Module ensures you never feel lost in the wrong way. Fog-of-war softening, landmark highlighting, and optional map telemetry give you the cartographer's edge without removing the thrill of discovery.

- Optional points of interest overlay layers.
- Adjustable discovery radius for obscured secrets.
- Time-of-day sensory presets to match your mood.
- Traversal heatmaps to visualize journey patterns.

### 📈 Progression Module

Growth should be legible. The Progression Module converts opaque advancement systems into transparent, tunable pipelines. Experience gain pacing, skill unlock visibility, and resource flow dashboards give you honest insight into how your hero evolves.

- XP curve visualization and optional pacing adjustments.
- Skill tree previews with dependency graphs.
- Inventory economy dashboards with trend lines.
- Milestone celebration toggles for narrative moments.

## ⚙️ Configuration Fundamentals

The companion config is a human-readable file organized into modular sections. Each section maps to a feature cluster, and each parameter carries a comment describing its role. You never need to guess — the defaults are safe, and every deviation is your deliberate choice.

A short summary of the structure:

- **Core Section** — foundational behavior and startup preferences.
- **Combat Section** — timings, feedback, and challenge tuning.
- **Movement Section** — traversal feel and camera related knobs.
- **Exploration Section** — map layers and discovery radius.
- **Progression Section** — advancement visibility and pacing.
- **Interface Section** — theming, scaling, and localization.

Profiles can be exported and imported as plain files, making it easy to share a curated experience with friends.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this safe for my saves?**
Yes. All changes are additive and reversible; nothing alters your save data destructively.

**Does it conflict with other tools?**
Generally no, but overlapping configuration hooks should be reviewed to avoid duplicate behavior.

**Can I revert everything easily?**
Absolutely. A single restore-default action rolls back every module.

**Is the interface available in my language?**
Likely — see the Multilingual Support section. If not, contributions are warmly welcomed.

**Do you gather telemetry?**
No personal telemetry is gathered. The companion works locally.
